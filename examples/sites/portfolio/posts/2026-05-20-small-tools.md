# Small tools, kept small

<Meta date="2026-05-20" tags="tools, craft" />

I maintain a handful of command line tools that each do one thing. People ask why I do not merge them into one. This is the answer, with numbers.

| Tool | Lines | Open issues | Years old |
|:-----|------:|------------:|----------:|
| tide | 1,840 | 2 | 4 |
| loupe | 960 | 0 | 3 |
| quay | 2,310 | 5 | 2 |

Each one is small enough to read in an afternoon. When one breaks, I know where to look, and when one is finished, I can stop.

The renderer from [a diff renderer in one evening](2026-09-12-a-diff-renderer-in-one-evening.md) started as part of *tide* and stayed there, because it did not need to be anywhere else.
